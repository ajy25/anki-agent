How do the search options differ?

Keyword search: leave both options off when you know a term or tag, such as sucralfate. The app searches locally.

AI assist: describe what you want, such as “cards about drugs that protect gastric ulcers.” AI explores your deck's tags and tries keywords, then builds a search. Results match that search; they are not individually screened for relevance.

Deep search: use a more specific request, such as “gastric ulcer treatments that protect the mucosa rather than suppress acid.” AI first finds possible matches, then reviews short card excerpts and keeps those it judges relevant, with a brief reason for each.

Deep search can take longer and use more API credits. For more than 120 candidates, you can narrow the request or continue; at most 400 candidates are reviewed per search. It can still miss relevant cards.

Choose one option at a time. Both AI options use your selected model. Expand Thinking in the results to inspect search activity and any reasoning text the model returns. Use Tutor when you want an explanation with card citations.
