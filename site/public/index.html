Full Mac setup guide ↗
↳

You’ll need Anki with your cards on this Mac, an internet connection, and your own Groq API key.

  1. 01

    Have your deck ready.

    Open Anki on your Mac and finish syncing the cards you want to study.

  2. 02

    Open the Mac app.

    Get the Mac installer, open the .dmg, and drag Anki Agent into Applications. Open it from there.

  3. 03

    Connect your Groq key.

    Create your own API key, paste it into Connect Groq in the app, and click Test key. Your key is saved in macOS Keychain.

  4. 04

    Pick a deck. You’re in.

    Choose your Anki profile and deck, then click Save & open deck. The tutor opens after your cards are prepared.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers
before you start.

Does this change my Anki cards?+

No. Anki Agent reads your collection to build a search index. It does not edit your cards or sync your Anki account.

Do I need to export my deck?+

No. The app looks for Anki profiles already on your Mac. Choose a profile and deck from the setup screen. If a profile is missing, use Browse to select its collection.anki2 file. The setup guide shows where to find it.

Where do my questions and cards go?+

Your collection and search index stay on your Mac. The tutor and AI search send your questions and relevant card text to Groq. Your API key is stored in macOS Keychain. AI features require an internet connection.

Is the app free?+

Anki Agent is open source under the MIT license. You bring your own Groq account and key; AI usage follows that account’s limits and billing settings.

What if macOS blocks the app?+

Preview builds are not notarized by Apple. If macOS cannot verify the developer and you trust the download, the first-open guide explains the per-app Open Anyway step. You do not need to turn off your Mac’s security protections.

Can I trust every AI answer?+

AI can make mistakes. Use the linked cards to check an explanation, and compare it with your study materials.
