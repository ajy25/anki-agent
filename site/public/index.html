Download and setup

For Macs with Apple silicon (Apple M-series chips). The app includes Python; you do not need Terminal or coding experience.

__RELEASE_STATUS__

__DOWNLOAD_LABEL__ Version __VERSION__ preview

  1. Have your Anki deck on this Mac. Open Anki and finish syncing your cards.
  2. Install Anki Agent. Download the .dmg from the release’s Assets, open it, and drag Anki Agent into Applications. Open it from there. Use the app download, rather than “Source code”.
  3. Add your Groq key. Create your own API key, paste it into Connect Groq in the app, and click Test key.
  4. Choose your cards. Select your Anki profile and deck, then click Save & open deck. Leave the window open while the deck is prepared.

Full Mac setup guide and troubleshooting — including what to do if macOS blocks the preview or your profile is missing.

Your Anki collection stays read-only. Your key is saved in macOS Keychain. AI requests send your questions and relevant card text to Groq, using your account’s limits and billing settings.
