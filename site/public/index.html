How the search options work

Keyword search: matches words and tags locally, without an AI call. Start here when you know a term, such as sucralfate.

AI assist: describe a topic. AI explores deck tags and tries keywords, then returns cards matching the search it builds.

Deep search: AI finds possible matches, screens short card excerpts for relevance, and gives a reason for each card kept. It uses more AI calls and may take longer. You can narrow broad searches; at most 400 candidates are reviewed per search.

Use Tutor for an explanation with card citations. Read the search guide.

Models and pricing

We recommend GPT-6 Luna on OpenRouter or GPT-OSS 120B on Groq for low-cost everyday studying. Luna has the lowest listed token prices; GPT-OSS 120B combines low prices with Groq’s fast inference. Both offer fast, streaming replies for quick back-and-forth studying.

Anki Agent is free. With these models, modest requests can cost fractions of a cent: a total of 10,000 input tokens and 1,000 output tokens costs about 0.15¢ with Luna or 0.21¢ with GPT-OSS 120B.
